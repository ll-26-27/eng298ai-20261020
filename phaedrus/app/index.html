Phaedrus, three ways

Plato’s dialogue (Stephanus 227–279) in Burnet’s Greek, Harold Fowler’s 1925 English, and two new translations made from the Greek in 1000-word pieces by ten Claude subagents: one into modern English, one into Korean. Compare them page by page, see where they diverge, and follow the key words.
