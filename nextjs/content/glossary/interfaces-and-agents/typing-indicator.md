# Typing indicator

**A visual signal that the interface is waiting for a reply.**

## In plain terms

Animated dots can make a pause look like someone composing an answer. The persona dial proposes switching that cue on and off while keeping the model fixed. Ask whether your reading of the pause changes.

An indicator is a presentation choice; it does not by itself show which computation is running.

## In the readings

In [§1 and §4.4](https://github.com/ll-26-27/eng-298ai-context/blob/24df8b8/_context/course-readings/course-readings-md/sacks-schegloff-jefferson-1974-turn-taking.md), Sacks, Schegloff, and Jefferson discuss transitions with little gap or overlap. As an interpretive exercise, ask how animated dots make a machine’s delay legible as a conversational pause.
