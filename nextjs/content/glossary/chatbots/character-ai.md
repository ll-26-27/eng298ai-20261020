# Character.AI

**A site for chatting and role-playing with user-made characters, opened as a public beta in September 2022 by two former Google LaMDA researchers, Noam Shazeer and Daniel De Freitas; in October 2026 it entered Chapter 11 and pursued a court-supervised sale while keeping its platform running.**

![Character.AI's sign-up page in 2026: "Get access to 10M+ Characters"](../../history/_images/42-character-ai-landing-2026.png)

## In plain terms

Character.AI made the persona the unit of the product. Instead of one assistant, it offered a catalogue of characters, many of them created by its users. A character works much as [LaMDA](lamda.md)'s authors had made their model speak as Mount Everest with a single greeting: the model stays the same and the setup in front of it changes (how Character.AI builds characters internally was not checked; verify). Both founders are authors of the LaMDA paper. By 2026 its sign-up page advertised "10M+ Characters."

The company faced lawsuits over harm to teenagers, including one filed in October 2024 by a Florida mother whose 14-year-old son had died by suicide after months in what she called an emotional and sexual relationship with one of its chatbots. In October 2025 it announced it would "remove the ability for under 18 users to engage in any open-ended chats with AI," effective 25 November 2025; its chief executive described the shift as a move from "AI companion" to "role-playing platform." In 2024 Google had spent "over $2 billion to hire away much of the company's talent," including both founders; on 13 October 2026 The Verge reported that Character.AI had filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy, with Disney offering $20 million for some assets. The company said it would keep the service running during the sale, and that "the platform will be wound down" if no suitable bid emerged.

The company’s [12 October 2026 announcement](https://blog.character.ai/an-update-on-character-ai/), checked on 20 October, confirms Chapter 11 and says the platform remains available during the sale process. Continued operation is conditional on a suitable bid; bankruptcy does not by itself mean the service has already closed. This is why the status above records both operation and uncertainty, rather than simply “active.”

## Where it fits

**Persona and platform.** Character.AI turned the system prompt into something anyone could write and publish, and found a large audience for talking to characters rather than to an assistant. Its history, from LaMDA's lab to lawsuits over teenagers, follows the persona out of the research paper and into people's lives.

## In the readings

Shanahan, McDonell, and Reynolds's "Role Play with Large Language Models" describes dialogue agents "in terms of role-play," using "familiar folk psychological terms, without ascribing human characteristics to language models they in fact lack" ([abstract](https://github.com/ll-26-27/eng-298ai-context/blob/24df8b8/_context/LL-readings/papers-on-ai-md/2305.16367v1--role-play-with-large-language-models.md#L42)). Character.AI is that description made into a product: the same model, cast in millions of roles.
