# Pi

**Inflection AI's "personal AI," released 2 May 2023 as a kind, supportive conversational companion rather than a productivity tool; in March 2024 two of its founders left to run Microsoft's consumer AI.**

## In plain terms

Inflection AI, founded in 2022 by Mustafa Suleyman (a co-founder of DeepMind), Karén Simonyan, and Reid Hoffman, launched Pi on 2 May 2023. The name stands for "personal intelligence," and the launch post defined it against the other chatbots: "The Pi experience is intended to prioritize conversations with people, where other AIs serve productivity, search, or answering questions. Pi is a coach, confidante, creative partner, or sounding board." Its listed traits read like a character sketch: "kind and supportive," "curious and humble," "creative and fun," "all yours." It was free, and reachable on the web, in an iPhone app, and by text message, WhatsApp, Instagram, and Messenger.

On 19 March 2024 Microsoft announced that Suleyman and Simonyan were "joining Microsoft to form a new organization called Microsoft AI, focused on advancing Copilot and our other consumer AI products and research," with Suleyman as its CEO. Inflection carried on without them. In July 2026 it described an "updated product experience" for Pi "with improvements to voice, conversation, and memory," and a new Pi Journeys experiment.

## Where it fits

**Persona and companionship.** Pi made the persona the product: not an assistant that also chats but a conversational partner whose temperament is the selling point. In 2024 Microsoft, which was already calling [Copilot](copilot.md) "your everyday AI companion," brought two of Pi's founders in to run the organization behind it.
