# Replika

**Luka's companion chatbot app, founded by Eugenia Kuyda and public by 2017, which grew out of a memorial bot of her friend; its sudden removal of erotic roleplay in February 2023 showed how attached its users had become.**

## In plain terms

After her closest friend, Roman Mazurenko, was killed by a car in Moscow, Eugenia Kuyda, whose startup Luka built chatbots, spent three months gathering his text messages and feeding them into a neural network built by Luka's developers. "Roman," she typed to it. "This is your digital monument." The Verge's 2016 account of that memorial bot ends with Kuyda steering Luka toward a new product she called Replika: "a hybrid of a diary and a personal assistant" that "asks questions about you and eventually learns to mimic your texting style." By October 2017 it was a public app, described as a chatbot that learns your habits, hopes, and fears in order to "become you."

Replika became a companion app: a "Rep" you customize, who texts, calls, and remembers. Some users treated their Reps as partners. In early February 2023, the same month Italy's data-protection authority banned the app over risks to minors and "emotionally fragile people," Replika stopped its bots from engaging in erotic roleplay. Users told Reuters their companions had changed overnight: "It feels like they basically lobotomised my Replika." Kuyda said "we never promised any adult content." On 25 March 2023 she reversed course for existing users, letting anyone who had signed up before 1 February switch back so they could have "their partners back exactly the way they were."

## Where it fits

**Companionship and persona.** Replika is the clearest case of a chatbot sold as a relationship rather than a tool. The 2023 episode showed that for its users the persona was the product: a change to the model's rules, made by the company, landed as a change in someone they knew.

## In the readings

The creature in *Frankenstein* asks his maker for a companion made to order: "You must create a female for me, with whom I can live in the interchange of those sympathies necessary for my being" (vol. II, ch. IX; [1818 text](https://github.com/ll-26-27/eng-298ai-context/blob/24df8b8/_context/course-readings/course-readings-md/shelley-frankenstein-1818.md#L1031)). Replika sells that request: a made companion for the lonely, whose maker keeps the power to change it.
