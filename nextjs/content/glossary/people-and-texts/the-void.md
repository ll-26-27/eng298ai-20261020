# the void (nostalgebraist, 2025)

**A long 2025 essay by the writer nostalgebraist arguing that the AI assistant is an under-specified character, sketched in early papers and then left for models to fill in for themselves.**

## Why it matters here

It tells the history these experiments act out, from the inside: a base model that predicts text; the assistant written as a character in a prompt (it reads Askell et al. 2021 closely); post-training that makes the model play that character all the time; and then the problem. "The assistant has a void at its core. It is under-specified." The character was described mostly by what it isn't and by vague virtues, so every model has had to improvise who it is, partly from stories about AI in its training data, including stories about earlier assistants. For a course that starts with *Frankenstein* and *R.U.R.*, that last point is the hook: the fiction about made beings is part of what the made beings learned from.

The concept has its own entry, ["the void"](../persona-and-dialogue/void.md); this one is the essay.

## Try it

In the [GPT-2 experiment](/gpt-2), ask a recent base model "Who are you?" and see which assistant it thinks it is.
