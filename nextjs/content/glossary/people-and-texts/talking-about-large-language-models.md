# Talking About Large Language Models (2022)

**Murray Shanahan's 2022 essay asking us to keep stepping back to how language models actually work before saying they "know," "believe," or "think."**

## Why it matters here

It is the precursor to the role-play paper and a good description of what the experiments are for. Shanahan's worry is that "the more adept LLMs become at mimicking human language, the more vulnerable we become to anthropomorphism," and that words like "knows" and "believes" make it worse; his remedy is "the practice of repeatedly stepping back to remind ourselves of how LLMs, and the systems of which they form a part, actually work." The phrase "the systems of which they form a part" matters here: a product like Claude or ChatGPT is a model plus a system prompt, memory, tools, and an interface, and what it "knows" may live in any of those.
