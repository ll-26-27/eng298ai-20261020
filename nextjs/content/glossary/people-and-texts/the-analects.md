# The Analects

**The sayings and exchanges of Confucius as his followers remembered them: a teacher who answers the particular person in front of him, recorded by others.**

## Why it matters here

The course reads the *Analects* beside the *Phaedrus* as one of two old models of teaching by dialogue, and the "historical chatbot" exercise makes it concrete: a Confucius bot is built from a book that, as Annping Chin puts it, "is not a record of what Confucius wrote, only of what he said," and said to particular people. A chatbot gives everyone the same teacher; the Confucius of the *Analects* deliberately does not.

Several sayings read like design notes for an assistant. Clever words and a pleasing manner are a warning sign (1.3), which is the problem of [sycophancy](../persona-and-dialogue/sycophancy.md). Saying you know only what you know (2.17) is the opposite of [hallucination](../persona-and-dialogue/hallucination.md). And the teacher who will not "repeat what I have done" for a student who doesn't bring back the other three corners (7.8) is the opposite of an assistant that always answers.

## In the readings

Cited from Annping Chin's translation (Penguin Classics, 2014), by book and passage:

- **1.3:** "A man of clever words and of a pleasing countenance is bound to be short on humanity" ([1.3](https://github.com/ll-26-27/eng-298ai-context/blob/24df8b8/_context/course-readings/course-readings-md/confucius-analects-chin.md?plain=1#L197)).
- **2.17:** "To say that you know something when you know it and to say that you do not know something when you do not know it—this is true knowing" ([2.17](https://github.com/ll-26-27/eng-298ai-context/blob/24df8b8/_context/course-readings/course-readings-md/confucius-analects-chin.md?plain=1#L350)).
- **5.10:** after Zai Yu, a good talker, disappoints him, Confucius stops trusting what people say and goes to "observe what they do" ([5.10](https://github.com/ll-26-27/eng-298ai-context/blob/24df8b8/_context/course-readings/course-readings-md/confucius-analects-chin.md?plain=1#L731)).
- **7.8:** "After I have shown a student one corner of a square, if he does not come back with the other three, I will not repeat what I have done" ([7.8](https://github.com/ll-26-27/eng-298ai-context/blob/24df8b8/_context/course-readings/course-readings-md/confucius-analects-chin.md?plain=1#L1047)).
- **11.22:** Zilu and Ran You ask the same question and get opposite answers, because "Qiu [Ran You] tends to hold back, and so I like to urge him on. You [Zilu] has the fire of two, and so I like to restrain him" ([11.22](https://github.com/ll-26-27/eng-298ai-context/blob/24df8b8/_context/course-readings/course-readings-md/confucius-analects-chin.md?plain=1#L1729)). Compare the *Phaedrus* on fitting speech to the kind of soul.
- **17.19:** "I wish not to speak anymore." Asked what his disciples would then transmit, he answers, "What does Heaven ever say?" ([17.19](https://github.com/ll-26-27/eng-298ai-context/blob/24df8b8/_context/course-readings/course-readings-md/confucius-analects-chin.md?plain=1#L2832)).

The syllabus lists it as recommended for October 6, "Dialogue: A short history."
