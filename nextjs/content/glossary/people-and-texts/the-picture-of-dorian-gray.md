# The Picture of Dorian Gray

**Oscar Wilde's novel of a young man who stays beautiful while his portrait records what he does: a persuasive surface kept apart from the history underneath.**

## Why it matters here

The course read it on September 22, and its central image keeps fitting the experiments: a face that stays the same while something hidden changes. A chat assistant presents one pleasant, unchanging face; what produced it (a base model, a training process, a long system prompt) is out of sight. The "shoggoth with a smiley face" meme is Dorian and his portrait drawn by the internet.

The novel is also about influence. Lord Henry shapes Dorian with talk, and a book poisons him; Lord Henry's line that an influenced person becomes "an actor of a part that has not been written for him" is close to how Shanahan, McDonell, and Reynolds describe a dialogue agent. And Sibyl Vane, the actress Dorian loves only while she performs, is a warning about loving the performance and not the performer.
