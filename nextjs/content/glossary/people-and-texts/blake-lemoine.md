# Blake Lemoine

**Google engineer who in June 2022 published a conversation with the LaMDA chatbot and said he believed it was sentient; Google placed him on leave and fired him the next month.**

## Why it matters here

Lemoine is the persona dial's 2022 stop on the anthropomorphization thread, between Weizenbaum's secretary and Bing's Sydney. The difference from 1966 is that he was a software engineer working on the system, testing it for bias, and the model was far more fluent; the likeness is that the conviction came from conversation. His published transcript, "Is LaMDA Sentient? — an Interview," is worth reading as a document: who asks the questions, what the questions assume, and how the model takes up the role it is offered. Read with the role-play paper, it looks like a model playing the sentient AI that the interview kept inviting.

The episode made "is it conscious?" the public question about chatbots months before ChatGPT launched.
