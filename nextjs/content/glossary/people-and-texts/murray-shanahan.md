# Murray Shanahan

**AI researcher at Google DeepMind and Imperial College London whose papers give the course a careful way to talk about language models without mistaking them for people.**

## Why it matters here

The syllabus names him at the outset, with Nina Beguš, as one of the theorists the course reads, and two of his papers are in the course collection: ["Talking About Large Language Models"](talking-about-large-language-models.md) (2022), which asks us to keep remembering how these systems actually work before saying they "know" or "believe," and [Role Play with Large Language Models](role-play-with-large-language-models.md) (2023, with Kyle McDonell and Laria Reynolds), the required reading for the chatbot workshop. Together they are the course's middle path between hype and dismissal: describe the performance precisely, in theatrical terms, and leave the question of a self open.

He has also worked on the fiction side: he was a scientific adviser to Alex Garland's film *Ex Machina* (2014), which drew on his book *Embodiment and the Inner Life* (2010).

## In the readings

The two papers above; the course copies are abstracts and notes in `LL-readings/papers-on-ai-md/`.
