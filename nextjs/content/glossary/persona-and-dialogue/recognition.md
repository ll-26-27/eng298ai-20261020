# Recognition

**Acknowledging another as a being whose claims or relations matter.**

## In plain terms

For this glossary, recognition is a humanities question about how someone comes to count as more than an object or a function. Keep it distinct from identifying a face or recognizing a word. When discussing a chatbot, specify what status is being granted and what warrants that judgment.

## In the readings

*Frankenstein*, Volume II, chapter II, makes a hearing of the creature’s account a condition of judgment. *R.U.R.*, Epilogue, turns on Alquist’s response to laughter, protection, and mutual sacrifice. These scenes invite a comparison of criteria for recognition; they do not establish software personhood.
