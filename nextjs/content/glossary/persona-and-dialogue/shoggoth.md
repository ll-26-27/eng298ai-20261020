# Shoggoth

**An AI meme depicting a strange creature behind a friendly face, used to question what an assistant persona reveals about its model.**

## In plain terms

The “shoggoth with a smiley face” image places a small friendly mask on a monstrous body. In AI discussions, the mask stands for the assistant presentation and the creature for a less familiar underlying model. This is a metaphor, not evidence that a model has a hidden self, intentions, or consciousness.

## In the readings

Read alongside [“Simulators”](../people-and-texts/simulators.md), which distinguishes a generator from its portrayed speakers. A course comparison with [The Picture of Dorian Gray](../people-and-texts/the-picture-of-dorian-gray.md) asks what the contrast between a visible face and a concealed portrait lets a reader imagine. The comparison is interpretive.

Ask which parts the mask metaphor explains and which it obscures.
