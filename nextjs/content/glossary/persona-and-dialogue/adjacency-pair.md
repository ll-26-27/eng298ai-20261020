# Adjacency pair

**A linked pair of conversational actions, such as a question and an answer.**

## In plain terms

A question makes an answer relevant; a greeting makes a return greeting relevant. A second contribution also shows how the first was understood. When ELIZA answers a complaint with another question, ask what action the reply performs rather than just whether it repeats the right words.

## In the readings

Sacks, Schegloff, and Jefferson, §4.12, discuss question–answer, greeting–greeting, and invitation–acceptance/decline sequences. In §6.3 they show how the next turn displays an understanding of the prior one. Two adjacent message bubbles need not form an adjacency pair.
