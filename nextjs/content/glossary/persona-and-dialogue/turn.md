# Turn

**An occasion for one participant to contribute to an exchange.**

## In plain terms

A turn need not be a whole sentence or an entire paragraph. A chatbot message often packages many sentences into one contribution, whereas a short “What?” can take a turn in spoken conversation. Ask what the interface lets the other participant do before the contribution finishes.

## In the readings

Sacks, Schegloff, and Jefferson, §3.1, describe word-, phrase-, clause-, and sentence-sized resources for constructing turns. Their §5 treats turn boundaries as interactionally determined. A message bubble is therefore an imperfect stand-in for a conversational turn.
