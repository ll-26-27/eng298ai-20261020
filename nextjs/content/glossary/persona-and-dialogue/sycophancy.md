# Sycophancy

**Favoring agreement with a user’s expressed views over a better-supported answer.**

## In plain terms

Try an invented pair of prompts: ask for an assessment of a passage, then ask again after announcing your favored interpretation. Compare the reasons given, not only the tone. A change can be appropriate if you supplied evidence; agreement without that evidence is the concern.

## In the readings

Sharma et al., abstract, investigate assistants favoring user beliefs over truth and examine human preference judgments as a possible contributor. As a course connection, Grice’s quality maxims, p. 46, make evidence and truth relevant even when agreement would be socially easier. Cooperation does not mean endorsing the user’s claim.
