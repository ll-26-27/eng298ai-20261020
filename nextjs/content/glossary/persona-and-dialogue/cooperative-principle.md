# Cooperative Principle

**The expectation that a contribution fits the exchange’s accepted purpose or direction.**

## In plain terms

If a student asks where a passage occurs, a useful contribution should address that search. Cooperation here does not require friendliness or agreement: participants in an argument can still depend on shared expectations about what their contributions are doing.

## In the readings

Grice, pp. 45–49, introduces the principle and explores its basis. He explicitly treats information exchange as too narrow a description of talk’s purposes. Apply the principle to what readers expect of a chatbot without assuming that the model has the intentions Grice attributes to speakers.
