# Few-shot prompting

**Providing a small set of examples in the prompt to establish a pattern.**

## In plain terms

Give an invented Course TF Assistant two short examples in which it asks a student to cite a passage, then supply a new student question. You are showing the desired exchange as well as describing it. Keep examples in the inspected input so the apparent spontaneous habit has a visible source.

## In the readings

The course’s “Role-Play with Large Language Models,” §2, describes example pairs as a way of eliciting continuations. Compare Shelley’s creature learning words by observing the cottagers in *Frankenstein*, Volume II, chapter IV. This is a comparison of learning scenes, not a claim that supplying examples in context is human development or weight training.
