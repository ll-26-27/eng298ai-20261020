# Implicature

**Something communicated beyond what the words explicitly say.**

## In plain terms

A reply may imply that a suggestion is impractical without saying so directly. To identify an implicature, explain how the wording, context, and expectations support your reading. With a chatbot, also ask whether the inference is yours rather than evidence of a hidden intention.

## In the readings

Grice, pp. 43–45, distinguishes saying from implicating; pp. 49–50 describes how an inference can be worked out. At pp. 57–58, conversational implicatures can be canceled and need not be part of the expression’s conventional meaning. Not every association a reader makes qualifies.
