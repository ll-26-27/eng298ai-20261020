# Flouting a maxim

**Conspicuously departing from a conversational maxim so a hearer can infer a further point.**

## In plain terms

An obviously inadequate answer can invite the hearer to search for an indirect message. That differs from secretly misleading someone, openly declining to contribute, or being unable to satisfy two expectations at once. Do not turn every chatbot failure into evidence of deliberate wit.

## In the readings

Grice, p. 49, explicitly separates these possibilities. His p. 52 recommendation-letter example exploits insufficient information to convey an unfavorable assessment. The distinction gives a more precise reading of an odd reply than simply calling it “uncooperative.”
