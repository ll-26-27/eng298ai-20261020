# Refusal

**A response that declines to carry out some or all of a request.**

## In plain terms

An invented Course TF Assistant can refuse to invent a page number and offer to search an available edition instead. Read the refusal both as a boundary and as part of the apparent character. A refusal’s wording alone does not identify which technical layer caused it.

## In the readings

Grice, p. 49, distinguishes opting out from covert violation and conspicuous flouting. This helps describe a stated limit without mistaking it for an indirect answer. *R.U.R.*, Epilogue, makes refusal of dissection and the protection of another into dramatic actions; that is a literary comparison, not a model of chatbot policy.
