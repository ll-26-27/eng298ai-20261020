# Hallucination

**Generated material presented as factual that is false or unsupported.**

## In plain terms

Use the term here for an invented citation or quotation offered as if it were checked. Distinguish it from openly labeled fiction: inventing dialogue for a literary exercise is a different act. With the Course TF Assistant, follow the citation to the passage before deciding the answer is grounded.

## In the readings

Grice’s quality maxims, p. 46, supply this course’s evaluative lens: avoid falsehood and assertions without adequate evidence. Plato, *Phaedrus*, 275a–b, distinguishes the appearance of wisdom from its possession. Neither author uses the modern technical label; the connection here is interpretive.
