# Repair

**The handling of trouble in speaking, hearing, understanding, or turn organization.**

## In plain terms

“Did you mean the narrator or the author?” opens a repair sequence. An apology alone does not show that the trouble has been resolved. With the Course TF Assistant, identify the misunderstanding, its attempted correction, and what the next response makes of that correction.

## In the readings

Sacks, Schegloff, and Jefferson, §4.14, discuss repair of overlap and other troubles, including correction within a turn. Their §6.3 explains how a later turn exposes an earlier misunderstanding. Repair is part of interaction’s organization, not just an error message.
