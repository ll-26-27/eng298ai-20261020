# Character

**A figure whose words and actions invite us to infer recurring dispositions.**

## In plain terms

A character is more than an accent or a list of adjectives: what the figure does under pressure matters. For an invented Course TF Assistant, compare its friendly introduction with its response when a student asks it to invent a quotation. The tension between its promises and conduct gives you something to interpret.

## In the readings

In *Dorian Gray*, chapters VII–VIII, beauty, performance, cruelty, and the altered portrait do not line up. Read the discrepancy as a warning against treating an attractive surface as a complete account of character. This literary comparison leaves open what kinds of character a chatbot can sustain.
