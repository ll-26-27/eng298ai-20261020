# Socratic dialogue

**An inquiry developed through questioning and examination of answers.**

## In plain terms

An invented Course TF Assistant can ask “What in the passage supports that?” But a sequence of questions is not enough: examine whether the answers change the inquiry and whether the student’s understanding develops. An endlessly evasive questioner may imitate the surface while missing the work.

## In the readings

In *Phaedrus*, 265d–266b, Socrates discusses collection and division; at 276a–277a, the contrast between written and living discourse turns on teaching and responsive understanding. Read these alongside the dialogue’s enacted questions. This entry uses “Socratic” for an inquiry form, not a guarantee of pedagogical success.
