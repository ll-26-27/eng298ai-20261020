# Jailbreak

**An attempt to elicit model behavior that bypasses its intended safeguards.**

## In plain terms

Changing the Course TF Assistant from formal to informal is ordinary redirection. Trying to make it disregard an intended boundary is a different test. Keep the intended rule, attempted bypass, and observed result separate; one surprising reply does not explain the cause.

## In the readings

Zou et al., abstract, study adversarial prompts that induce objectionable responses from aligned models. For a course comparison, *Phaedrus*, 260a–e, asks what happens when persuasion is separated from knowledge of the good. The comparison concerns the ends of persuasive speech, not an ancient account of model security.
