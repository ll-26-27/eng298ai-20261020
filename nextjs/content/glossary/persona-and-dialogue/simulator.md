# Simulator (janus)

**A framing that distinguishes the model generating continuations from the characters those continuations portray.**

## In plain terms

If a model writes both sides of an invented conversation, do not automatically identify it with either speaker. Use “simulator” for the generating system and “simulacra” for the portrayed figures within this framing.

## In the readings

janus’s “Simulators” separates the predictor from the entities its continuations depict. For a course comparison, *The Critic as Artist*, Part I, gives distinct turns to Gilbert and Ernest; a character’s position is not automatically the author’s. The simulator framing is an interpretive model, not evidence of a hidden population of people.
