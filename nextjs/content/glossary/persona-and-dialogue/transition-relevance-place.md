# Transition-relevance place

**A point of possible completion where a change of speaker becomes relevant.**

## In plain terms

When a question sounds complete, another person may begin. That opportunity need not mean the speaker must stop forever. Compare this with a chatbot answer: the visible end of one sentence may offer no control for taking the floor.

## In the readings

Sacks, Schegloff, and Jefferson, §3.1–3.4, locate turn transfer at possible completions of turn-constructional units. The point is projected within an unfolding interaction, not mechanically equated with every period on a page.
