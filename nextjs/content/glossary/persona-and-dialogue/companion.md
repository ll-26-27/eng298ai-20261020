# Companion

**A speaking part organized around ongoing company rather than a single completed task.**

## In plain terms

For this course, compare a Course TF Assistant that helps locate a passage with an invented companion that asks how the student’s day went. The contrast concerns the relationship being staged. Friendly wording alone cannot establish mutual care or a shared life.

## In the readings

In *Frankenstein*, Volume II, chapter II, the creature appeals to his creator’s obligations and describes exclusion from human company. In *R.U.R.*, Epilogue, mutual protection matters to Alquist’s recognition of Primus and Helena. These are literary questions to bring to companion designs, not evidence that a chatbot experiences loneliness.
