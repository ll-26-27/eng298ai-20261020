# Interiority

**The sense of an inner life beyond a figure’s outward words and actions.**

## In plain terms

A character may seem to have thoughts it has not said. In a chatbot, first-person narration can invite the same reading. Treat the narration as something to interpret and verify where possible; its existence alone does not establish an enduring private experience behind it.

## In the readings

*Frankenstein*, Volume II, chapter III, gives the creature a retrospective account of sensation and becoming aware. *Dorian Gray*, chapter VII, separates public appearance from a concealed moral record in the portrait. These literary devices provide ways to study how an inside becomes imaginable, not models of a chatbot’s architecture.
