# ELIZA effect

**Attributing understanding or a personal presence to a conversational system on the strength of its replies.**

## In plain terms

In the familiar “Men are all alike” exchange, the user can supply a meaningful situation around responses produced by textual rules. Inspect the rule and then reread the reply: does it still feel like attention? The question concerns the user’s interpretation as well as the mechanism.

## In the readings

Weizenbaum’s ELIZA paper, pp. 36–37, presents the exchange and the problem of apparently understanding conversation. For a comparison, Plato’s *Phaedrus*, 275d–e, describes written words that seem intelligent but cannot explain themselves when questioned. A chatbot’s ability to vary replies changes the problem; it does not settle understanding.
