# Recipient design

**Shaping a contribution for the particular person or people being addressed.**

## In plain terms

An invented Course TF Assistant might explain the same passage differently to a first-time reader and to someone drafting a dissertation. Test whether its choices respond to the learner’s stated difficulty, rather than merely adding the learner’s name.

## In the readings

Sacks, Schegloff, and Jefferson, §5, locate recipient design in word choice, topics, sequences, and the organization of turns. Plato, *Phaedrus*, 271a–272b, similarly requires the rhetorician to connect kinds of speech with kinds of hearers. The traditions differ, but both make the recipient consequential.
