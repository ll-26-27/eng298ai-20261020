# Role-play

**Producing speech as a specified or emerging character.**

## In plain terms

Ask the Course TF Assistant to explain a passage as an impatient examiner, then compare the performance with its ordinary answers. Describe the changed conduct before deciding what kind of speaker it implies.

## In the readings

Shanahan, McDonell, and Reynolds propose role-play as a vocabulary that avoids literal attribution of human characteristics (abstract). Wilde’s *Dorian Gray*, chapter VII, supplies a literary counterpoint: Sibyl’s acting and her personal attachment pull apart.
