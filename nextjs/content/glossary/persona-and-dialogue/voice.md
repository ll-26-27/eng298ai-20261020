# Voice

**The qualities of expression through which speech becomes recognizable as a particular speaker’s.**

## In plain terms

Voice can mean audible sound or a recognizable textual manner. Here, compare wording, rhythm, habitual qualifications, and forms of address. A Course TF Assistant can sound formal without acquiring a new biography; an audio performance adds timing and sound to the comparison.

## In the readings

In *Dorian Gray*, chapter VII, Sibyl’s beautiful voice does not rescue her unconvincing performance. *The Critic as Artist*, Part I’s opening scene, also stages a move from music to talk. These scenes separate verbal content, delivery, and the audience’s impression of a speaker.
