# Turn-taking

**The organization of who speaks next and when.**

## In plain terms

Taking turns is more than alternating two labels. Participants can select another speaker, start on their own, or continue if no one else starts. With ELIZA, inspect how the interface narrows those possibilities into submitting a message and receiving a reply.

## In the readings

Sacks, Schegloff, and Jefferson, §3.3–3.4, give ordered rules for next-speaker selection, self-selection, and continuation. Their ordering helps explain coordinated transitions. Use those rules to notice what a chat interface simplifies; do not assume button-based alternation reproduces the spoken system.
