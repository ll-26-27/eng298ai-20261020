# Parasocial relationship

**A felt relationship with a mediated persona that lacks the mutual obligations of an ordinary interpersonal relationship.**

## In plain terms

Horton and Wohl describe audiences feeling personally addressed by a broadcast performer. Familiarity can accumulate across encounters without the performer knowing each audience member. Applying the term to chatbots is a comparison: a chatbot responds to individual messages, unlike a broadcast, but responsiveness alone does not establish mutual care or obligation.

## In the readings

For a course discussion, compare this distinction with [recognition](recognition.md): what does a user feel, and what capacities do they attribute to the other participant? Treat those as separate questions.
