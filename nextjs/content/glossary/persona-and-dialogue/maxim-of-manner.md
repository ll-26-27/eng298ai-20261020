# Maxim of manner

**The expectation that a contribution is expressed clearly, briefly, and in an orderly way.**

## In plain terms

Two explanations can carry similar information while differing in ambiguity and organization. Ask the Course TF Assistant to define the same term for two readers, then inspect whether its wording clarifies the idea or merely sounds scholarly.

## In the readings

Grice, p. 46, distinguishes manner—how something is said—from the other categories. On pp. 55–56, an elaborate description in place of a simple verb can itself invite an implicature. Style is therefore part of meaning in an exchange, not just decoration.
