# Mechanical rigidity

**In Bergson’s account of comedy, inflexibility where living responsiveness is expected.**

## In plain terms

Imagine the Course TF Assistant repeating the same cheerful introduction after every correction. The words may be pleasant while the pattern feels unresponsive. This is a proposed reading exercise: identify the recurring mechanism and the situation to which it fails to adapt.

## In the readings

Bergson, *Laughter*, chapter I, §II, moves from stumbling and practical jokes to rigidity of habit. Chapter I, §V, extends mechanical effects into social forms. Applying this to chatbot repetitions is an interpretive analogy; laughter does not reveal a system’s technical mechanism or decide whether it is alive.
