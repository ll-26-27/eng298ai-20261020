# Turn-constructional unit

**A stretch of talk that can constitute a possibly complete turn.**

## In plain terms

A single word can be enough: after “Who wants to go first?”, “Me” may complete a turn. A longer explanation may project a completion point only after its clause or sentence. Treat the unit as something participants can anticipate, rather than a fixed number of words.

## In the readings

Sacks, Schegloff, and Jefferson, §3.1 and §4.13, describe the unit-types and their projectability. Their account gives a way to compare a student’s brief reply with a Course TF Assistant’s multi-paragraph answer without treating both merely as one bubble.
