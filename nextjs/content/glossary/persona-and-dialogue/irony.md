# Irony

**Speech whose intended point can diverge sharply from its apparent literal assertion.**

## In plain terms

After a failed answer, an invented “Excellent work” may be praise or criticism. Context and tone matter. When a persona sounds sarcastic, distinguish a recognizable style from a successful inference about what the exchange means.

## In the readings

Grice, p. 53, explains a case of irony through a conspicuous departure from quality. In Wilde’s *Dorian Gray*, chapter VII, Lord Henry’s remarks about acting and love also demand attention to speaker, occasion, and audience. Neither passage licenses treating every false statement as ironic.
