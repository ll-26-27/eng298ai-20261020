# Rhetoric

**The shaping of speech to address and persuade an audience.**

## In plain terms

The Course TF Assistant’s assurance, ordering of evidence, and examples can make an answer persuasive. Evaluate how the answer earns assent as well as whether it is correct. Persuasiveness and the possession of evidence are separate questions.

## In the readings

Plato, *Phaedrus*, 259e–260e, tests rhetoric’s relation to truth; 271a–272b connects speech with the hearer; 273d–274a returns to likeness and truth. A smooth chatbot answer offers a practical occasion for these questions, not a shortcut to resolving Plato’s argument.
