# Grice’s maxims

**Expectations about information, evidence, relevance, and expression that help hearers interpret talk.**

## In plain terms

Quantity concerns how much is supplied; quality concerns truth and evidence; relation concerns relevance; manner concerns expression. They help explain why a reader searches for a point behind an apparently odd reply. They are not simply four instructions for making a chatbot polite.

## In the readings

Grice, pp. 45–47, presents the four categories, and pp. 49–50 distinguishes covert violation, opting out, clashes, and conspicuous flouting. That distinction matters: not every unhelpful response is an indirect message waiting to be decoded.
