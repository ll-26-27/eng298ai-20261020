# Anthropomorphism

**Interpreting something nonhuman through human attributes or capacities.**

## In plain terms

Calling a chatbot “hurt” after a refusal attributes more than a pattern of words. In the persona dial, record what changed in the visible response and what you inferred about the speaker. Keeping those records separate makes the inference available for examination.

## In the readings

The role-play paper’s abstract explicitly proposes a vocabulary intended to avoid anthropomorphism. In *R.U.R.*, Act One, Helena challenges Domin’s classification of robots; in the Epilogue, Alquist reconsiders what he encounters. Use the contrast to investigate recognition, not to assume that the play’s living beings and software share a status.
