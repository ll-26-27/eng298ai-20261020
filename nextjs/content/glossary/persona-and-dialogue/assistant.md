# Assistant

**The service-oriented speaking part that answers or helps the user.**

## In plain terms

“Assistant” names a role within an exchange. It is already a characterization, even when there is no fictional name or costume. In the four-column experiment, ask which outputs take responsibility for answering the question and which merely continue the surrounding text.

## In the readings

Askell et al. frame the desired assistant through helpfulness, honesty, and harmlessness (abstract). For a humanities comparison, *R.U.R.*, Act One, makes the design of a useful worker a consequential choice about which capacities its makers value. The play’s biological robots are not language models.
