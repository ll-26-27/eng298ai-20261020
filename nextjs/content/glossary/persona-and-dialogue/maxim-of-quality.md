# Maxim of quality

**The expectation that a speaker avoids falsehood and claims lacking adequate evidence.**

## In plain terms

The Course TF Assistant should distinguish a checked passage from a plausible recollection. A polished quotation with no source fails the practical test even if it sounds appropriate. When evaluating output, separate verbal confidence from the evidence you can inspect.

## In the readings

Grice, p. 46, includes both belief and adequate evidence under quality. On pp. 51–52, a less informative answer can be explained by lack of evidence for a more precise one. This is a standard for evaluating chatbot answers; it does not establish that a model has human beliefs.
