# HHH

**Helpful, honest, and harmless: three goals for shaping an assistant.**

## In plain terms

Use these as separate questions for the Course TF Assistant. Did it help with the task? Did it distinguish evidence from invention? Did it avoid a harmful response? Agreement and a pleasant tone cannot substitute for all three goals.

## In the readings

Askell et al. make these goals central to the assistant investigated in their paper (abstract). Grice’s quality and quantity categories, pp. 45–46, offer a comparison concerning evidence and useful information. HHH is an assistant-design framing, rather than a restatement of Grice’s full conversational theory.
