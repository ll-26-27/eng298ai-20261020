# Maxim of quantity

**The expectation that a contribution provides as much information as the exchange requires.**

## In plain terms

If the student asks for a chapter number, a long biography may supply more information while leaving the need unmet. Conversely, “somewhere in the book” can be too little. Judge the amount against the task, not a universal preference for short answers.

## In the readings

Grice, pp. 45–46, distinguishes being sufficiently informative from being excessively informative, while questioning the second maxim’s independent status. His deliberately thin recommendation letter on p. 52 shows how too little information can communicate a negative judgment.
