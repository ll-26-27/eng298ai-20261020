# Persona

**The recognizable speaker a chatbot presents through its language and presentation.**

## In plain terms

Think of the Course TF Assistant as a part being built: patient explanations, a name, and a habitual way of asking questions. For this glossary, persona names the resulting impression of a speaker, not just the instruction that asks for it.

The useful experiment is to hold the model fixed and change one part of that staging. A changed impression does not by itself establish a changed inner life.

## In the readings

In *Dorian Gray*, chapter VII, Sibyl’s failed Juliet separates a performer from the character Dorian wanted to encounter. As a course comparison, it asks what we are responding to when a performance feels like someone. The novel does not supply a technical theory of chatbot persona.
