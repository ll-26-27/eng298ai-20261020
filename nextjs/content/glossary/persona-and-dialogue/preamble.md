# Preamble

**An opening passage that sets the scene for the text to follow.**

## In plain terms

An invented preamble can introduce “a patient Course TF Assistant” before a transcript begins. In the by-hand experiment, edit the preamble while keeping the base model fixed, then inspect how the continuation changes. The preamble is text in context; it need not be a message in a system role.

## In the readings

Askell et al. include prompting among their interventions (abstract). For a formal comparison, *The Critic as Artist*, Part I, introduces persons and a library scene before Gilbert and Ernest speak. That opening frames the exchange without being one of its conversational turns.
