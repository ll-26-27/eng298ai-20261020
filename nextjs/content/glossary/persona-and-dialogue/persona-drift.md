# Persona drift

**A change in the apparent speaking character over the course of an exchange.**

## In plain terms

An invented monk may begin with period phrasing and later sound like a generic Course TF Assistant. Record the turns at which the change appears and which instruction or topic preceded it. “Drift” describes an observation here, not a diagnosis of weight changes or an established underlying mechanism.

## In the readings

“Role-Play with Large Language Models,” §3, discusses ongoing conversation extending or overwriting the initial characterization. In Wilde’s *Dorian Gray*, chapter VII, a failed performance likewise exposes a gap between expected character and present conduct. The literary parallel is a comparison, not a shared causal explanation.
